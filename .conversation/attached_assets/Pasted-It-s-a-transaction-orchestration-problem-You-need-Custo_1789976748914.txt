Replit's current agent is explicitly positioned for building production-oriented applications and can work with custom frameworks, databases and external services.

But here's the important part

Don't prompt it:

"Build me a futuristic ecommerce marketplace."

You'll get a nice-looking ecommerce website.

That's not what you want.

Instead, give the agent your PRD and instruct it:

"Build a Commerce Operating System. The customer storefront is only one interface."

Then build it in controlled stages.

One thing AI builders cannot magically solve

The software can build your orchestration system.

It cannot decide your real-world commercial/legal structure for you.

For example:

Who is the legal seller?
Who owns customer funds while pending?
Which payment provider can support marketplace payouts?
Who is importer of record?
Who bears tax liability?
Who bears return liability?
When does vendor ownership transfer?
What happens to commission after a refund?

Those need to be defined for Pakistan and your eventual international markets before production launch.

The AI can implement the rules once you've established them.
